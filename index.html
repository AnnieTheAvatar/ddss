Understanding the data

Definitions and scope used throughout these comparisons.

General notes

Student population
Domestic onshore students only, at Table A and Table B providers, across all course levels unless undergraduate is specified.
Rate
The indicator expressed as a percentage of all domestic onshore students.
EFTSL
Equivalent full-time study load.

Equity groups

Non-English Speaking Background (NESB)
A domestic student who arrived in Australia less than 10 years before the data was collected, from a home where a language other than English is spoken.
Disability
A student who has indicated one or more disability, impairment, or long-term medical condition that may affect their studies.
Regional/Remote
Derived from the ABS's Australian Statistical Geography Standard (MCEETYA 2006 classification), assigned by postcode.
Low SES
Based on the ABS SEIFA Index of Education and Occupation; students whose home postcode falls in the bottom 25% of areas nationally.

Metrics

Access Rate
Commencing students in the equity group divided by all commencing domestic onshore students.
Retention Rate
Retained students divided by (all enrolled students minus completed students); measures continuation from one year to the next.
Success Rate
EFTSL passed divided by EFTSL attempted (passed plus failed plus withdrawn).
Attainment Rate
Award course completions by the equity group divided by all domestic award course completions.
